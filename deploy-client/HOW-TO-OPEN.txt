============================================================

2) Open the HTTPS link Netlify gives you
3) Admin CMS: /admin/   user=admin   pass=shumail2026

Local preview (on this PC):
  python -m http.server 8765
  open http://127.0.0.1:8765/

Hero (full-width, black bg, no over-zoom — object-fit contain):
  assets/hero/best-01.jpg … best-08.jpg  stills

  Replace those files to change the top live showcase.

Creatives grid: Admin → Creatives / data/gallery.json

hero live-*.mp4 clips stay in assets/hero/. Posters still show in grid.
